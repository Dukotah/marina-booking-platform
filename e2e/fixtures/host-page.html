Hours

Summer: 7am–7pm daily. Off season: Friday to Sunday, 8am–5pm.

Last boat out is two hours before close, no exceptions, sorry.

The fleet

Two 22ft pontoons, a 19ft bowrider, six sit-on-top kayaks and a pair of paddleboards that are older than most of the people who rent them. Everything is inspected on the trailer before it goes in the water and again when it comes out.

Life jackets are included and are not optional. We carry infant through adult XXL. If you are bringing a dog, tell us when you book so we put the boat with the swim step on your reservation.

Fuel is not included on the powerboats. There is a pump at the top of the ramp and the nearest gas station is eleven miles back toward Geyserville, so top up before you drive out.

Wind and water

The lake sits in a canyon, so the afternoon wind funnels down the main body almost every day from about two o'clock. It is not dangerous but it will make a kayak trip home twice as long as the trip out. Plan your paddling for the morning and save the powerboat for the afternoon.

Water level is managed by the Corps of Engineers and moves a lot between May and September. When it is low, the courtesy dock on the south side is out of the water and everybody uses the main ramp, which backs up on Saturdays. Booking a morning slot is the whole trick.

We close the ramp when the surface wind is over 25 knots sustained. If that happens on your day we move your booking or refund it, your choice, no argument and no fee.

Finding us

Take Dry Creek Road past the last vineyard and follow the signs for the public ramp. We are the second driveway on the right after the cattle guard, past the row of eucalyptus. Cell service dies about a mile before you get here, so load your directions before you leave town.

Parking is free for renters and $12 for everybody else. Trailer parking is at the top of the hill; leave yourself ten minutes to walk back down.

There is no restaurant. There is a vending machine, a hose, a fish cleaning station and a very old dog named Pilot who will follow you to the dock.

The fine print

Cancel more than 48 hours out for a full refund. Inside 48 hours we will move you to another day at no charge. No shows are charged in full, because the boat sat on the dock all day and somebody else wanted it.

Whoever is driving needs to be 18 with a photo ID and has to sign the waiver before the boat leaves the dock. California boater cards are checked. If you have never driven a boat, say so and we will run you through it; it takes ten minutes and it is free.

We do not rent to anyone who has been drinking. This is not negotiable and it is the one rule we will keep your money over.
